# Science lesson-planning pedagogy

Read this file when the new lesson's primary subject is science.

## Three-dimensional learning

Make the dimensions visible:

- **Science and Engineering Practice (SEP):** what students do to build/use knowledge;
- **Disciplinary Core Idea (DCI):** the content model;
- **Crosscutting Concept (CCC):** the lens, such as cause/effect, systems, patterns, energy/matter.

Do not collapse them into a vague “students understand science” objective.

## Phenomenon-first rule

Use a specific phenomenon/question students can explain better by the end. **Investigation before explanation.** Do not tell the mechanism in the launch.

Common arc:

`Phenomenon → Investigation → Sensemaking/Argument → CER/Explanation → Model Revision → Formative Check`

## Grade bands

### K–2

Use directly observable phenomena and hands-on exploration. Students record by drawing, labels, measurement, oral language. Models explain what may be happening, including things they infer but cannot directly see.

### 3–5

Investigation generates evidence. Students begin explicit Claim–Evidence–Reasoning. Models become mechanistic: how/why, not merely labeled diagrams. Use a new mini-phenomenon for transfer on the exit ticket.

### 6–8

Quantitative reasoning matters where data are numeric. Students cite actual values, compare explanations, and revise models. Argumentation requires evidence from the investigation, not “I already knew it.”

### 9–12

Use mathematical/computational models when relevant, extended investigation, competing models/explanations, explicit uncertainty, and substantive science–society/ethical reasoning. An equation must connect to the physical/biological mechanism it models.

## Curriculum boundary

Only use OpenSciEd-specific terms when OpenSciEd is confirmed by the teacher/material. If confirmed, design an original complementary lesson; do not reproduce an upstream OpenSciEd lesson.

## Look-fors

Investigation look-fors identify student observations/data representations and teacher moves. Discussion look-fors identify evidence use, model changes, and use of the relevant CCC.

## Exit / Formative Gate

The check should require students to use today's DCI with the foregrounded SEP/CCC, often through explanation, model revision, or transfer to a fresh case. A response that only repeats vocabulary is insufficient.
